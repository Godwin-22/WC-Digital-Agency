Good to Know

Frequently asked questions

Will this work for my niche?

Yes. Our intake brief captures your audience, your offer, your price points and the problems you solve, so the copy and scripts are written specifically for your market.

Do I need to be making sales already?

This offer is built for businesses that already make sales organically — through referrals, WhatsApp, social media or their network. If you are yet to make your first sales, we recommend waiting until you have a few.

Do the prices include ad spend?

No. You pay ad spend directly to the platforms through your own ad account. The Ad Setup Guide includes budget guidelines so you launch with confidence.

Can my team or assistant launch the DIY Kit?

Yes. It comes with finished assets and a step-by-step guide that any capable team member can follow.

How does the Done-for-You Pack work?

We write your custom copy and scripts, produce your finished creatives and prepare your campaign structure. You review, approve and deploy — or add our ad management service to run the ads for you.

Do I need to appear on camera?

No. The video scripts are written as voice-overs, so you can produce them with footage, graphics and recorded audio.

What if you miss the 72 hours?

We refund your delivery fee in full. That's our guarantee.

What happens after I apply?

You submit a short form and land on WhatsApp with our team. We confirm your fit and package, then send your creative brief — which starts the 72-hour clock once approved.
